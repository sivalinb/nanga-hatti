# Name, context and illustration sources

**Nanga Hatti** is the name selected by Siva Babu. Bellie Jayaprakash's community site lists [“Nanga – We”](https://badaga.co/nanga-we/) and [identifies Hatti as the Badaga word for village](https://badaga.co/2021/06/08/the-beauty-of-ha-sound-in-badaga/). “Our village, remembered” is the project's English tagline, not a verified literal translation of the combined name. Romanization and usage can vary; this project does not claim community-wide endorsement or generate Badaga translations or pronunciation.

These links are sources and potential starting points for collaboration. No partnership, content license, endorsement or contributor agreement is implied. Personal memories remain labeled as accounts; source context does not prove them. Material about the wider Nilgiri Biosphere Reserve can include regions beyond the district in Tamil Nadu.

The region map uses an attributed OpenStreetMap geometry snapshot retrieved via Overpass on 13 October 2026. It includes selected roads, rivers and water polygons. See `static/region.geojson` metadata and `static/MAP-ATTRIBUTION.txt`. Public town markers are approximate town-level orientation, not exact community, home, spring or sacred-site coordinates. The map is not a navigation or hazard product. Map data retains ODbL 1.0; Leaflet 1.9.4 retains its bundled license.

The cover illustration in `static/assets/hills.webp` was generated with the built-in Imagegen tool. It portrays an imaginative Nilgiris-inspired landscape and fictional people in ordinary clothing; it is not documentary evidence about a named village or community. The actual app and dashboard screenshots are labeled separately. The generation prompt is preserved in [illustration-prompt.txt](illustration-prompt.txt).

No third-party recordings, photographs, dictionary pages or archive objects are redistributed. Software's MIT license does not establish rights to future contributed media. Contributors must decide what can be saved and shared; their original words remain intact.
